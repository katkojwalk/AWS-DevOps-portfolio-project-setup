Thank you for installing the frontend chart.

To access your application:
  1. If ingress is enabled and an ALB controller is installed, retrieve the ALB DNS from:
     kubectl get ingress -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

  2. Or forward port locally:
     kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "frontend.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

To uninstall:
  helm uninstall {{ .Release.Name }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
